Workplace Childcare in Malaysia: An Employer Guide

JKM requirements, LHDN tax incentives, and the options that work if you want to help employees with childcare.

The short answer

A workplace taska (taska tempat kerja) is a childcare centre an employer sets up for employees' children. It cares for 10 or more children under 4 years old and must be registered with JKM, at a fee of RM250 for 5 years.

Employers can claim tax incentives on the building and running costs. If you have no space, the simpler option is a childcare allowance, which is tax-exempt for employees up to RM3,000 a year.

Three options for employers

🏢 Your own taska

A taska on your premises, registered as a workplace taska. The closest option for employees, but the highest cost and responsibility.

🤝 Partner with a taska

Reserve places or negotiate a rate with a registered taska nearby, and pay part of the fees.

💵 Childcare allowance

Pay an allowance to employees or directly to the taska. The easiest to start, and suits smaller companies.

Start with a short survey: how many employees have children under 4, and how many would actually use a taska at work. A workplace taska needs at least 10 children. If demand is smaller, the second or third option usually makes more sense.

JKM requirements for a workplace taska

  • Registration under the Child Care Centre Act 1984 (Act 308). Fee RM250, certificate valid for 5 years. JKM processes a complete application within 21 working days.
  • Floor space of at least 3.5 m² per child.
  • Support letters from the local authority (PBT), the Fire and Rescue Department (Bomba) and the Health Department, plus a floor plan and location plan.
  • CCTV must be installed (required for all taska except home taska).
  • Staff must pass the JKM-recognised PERMATA childcare course (KAP) within 12 months of starting work.
  • Daily schedule, menu and taska rules submitted with the application.

Tax incentives for employers

LHDN Public Ruling No. 5/2016 (Tax Incentives for Employers Who Provide Child Care Centres) lets employers claim:

  • A 10% annual Industrial Building Allowance on the cost of a building built or bought and used as a childcare centre for employees' children.
  • A tax deduction for the cost of providing and maintaining the centre, such as rent, salaries, food and cleaning.
  • A tax deduction for childcare allowances paid to employees.

The centre must be registered with JKM under Act 308 to qualify.

For employees: since year of assessment 2024, a childcare allowance is tax-exempt up to RM3,000 a year (previously RM2,400), for children aged 12 and below. This includes allowances the employer pays directly to the childcare centre.

⚠️ Please verify: this is based on LHDN Public Ruling 5/2016 and the Budget 2024 tax measures, checked in October 2026. A new Budget can change rates and conditions. Confirm with LHDN or your tax agent before deciding.

7 steps to set up a workplace taska

  1. Survey employee demand. The number of children under 4, their ages, and parents' working hours (including shifts).
  2. Choose an operating model. Run it yourself with your own staff, or appoint an experienced taska operator to run it for you.
  3. Find the space. Allow 3.5 m² per child, plus room for sleeping, a kitchen, toilets and an emergency exit route.
  4. Get support letters from the technical agencies: the local authority, Bomba and the Health Department.
  5. Hire and train staff. Follow the caregiver ratios, and make sure everyone passes the PERMATA childcare course (KAP) within 12 months.
  6. Apply to JKM through your District Social Welfare Office, with the RM250 fee and supporting documents.
  7. Open and renew. Registration is valid for 5 years. Start renewing 3 months before it expires.

More detail on registration is in our TASKA Registration Guide, and start-up cost estimates in Cost of Opening a TASKA (both in Malay).

Important: a workplace taska is set up for employees' children. If you plan to accept children from the public as well, ask your District Social Welfare Office whether the institutional category fits better before applying.

Frequently asked questions

What is a workplace taska?

A childcare centre set up by an employer for the welfare of its employees' children. It takes 10 or more children under 4 years old and must be registered with JKM.

How much does it cost to register a workplace taska?

RM250 for 5 years. The real cost includes renovating the space, CCTV, caregiver salaries and the PERMATA childcare course (KAP).

What tax incentives do employers get for providing childcare?

A 10% annual Industrial Building Allowance on the building, a deduction for running costs, and a deduction for childcare allowances paid to employees, under LHDN Public Ruling 5/2016. Confirm with LHDN or your tax agent.

What if we have no space for a childcare centre?

Pay a childcare allowance. Employees are tax-exempt up to RM3,000 a year for children aged 12 and below, including when it is paid directly to the taska.

Can a workplace taska accept children from outside the company?

It is set up for employees' children. Ask your District Social Welfare Office about the right category if you want to accept the public.
